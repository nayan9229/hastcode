Last updated: 27 October 2026.

HastCode has no accounts and no comments. You can read every page without
telling us who you are. This page lists what is collected when you visit,
who collects it, and what you can do about it.

## Measuring visits: Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are read, roughly
where Readers are (country and city level), which site sent them, what
device and browser they use, and the words typed into our search box.
Google Analytics sets cookies on hastcode.com (named `_ga` and
`_ga_<id>`) to tell one visit from the next. We use these numbers to decide
what to write and to show advertisers how many people read the site. We
never receive your name or email from it.

## Advertising: Google AdSense

Pages carry ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use
cookies to serve ads, to measure them and, where you allow it, to
personalise them based on your visits to this and other sites. Google
explains how it uses information from sites like this one at
[policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can turn off personalised ads from Google at
[myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com/), and from many
other ad companies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or
Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before any
advertising or analytics cookie is set. Until you agree, Google Analytics
sets no cookies and receives only cookieless signals that a page was
viewed. You can change your choice at any time through the privacy
settings link that Google's message places on the page.

## The newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address and the page you subscribed from go
to [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy), which sends the
newsletter for us. You confirm by email before anything is sent, and every
issue has an unsubscribe link. To stop abuse, the sign-up form counts
attempts per IP address for ten minutes in memory; the count is never
stored.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes each request (including
your IP address) to deliver the page and protect the site from attacks.

## On your device only

If you switch between light and dark mode, the choice is saved in your
browser's local storage so the next page matches. It never leaves your
device.
