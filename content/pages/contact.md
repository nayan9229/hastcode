HastCode is written and edited by the HastCode editorial team under the
house byline [Mira Vance](/authors/mira-vance/). Every message is read by
the team.

## Email

Write to the team at
[mira-vance@hastcode.com](mailto:mira-vance@hastcode.com).

Please use the subject line to say what your message is about:

- **Correction:** the page's address and what is wrong, with a link to a
  first-party source if you have one. See how we handle corrections in the
  [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).
- **Tip:** something that happened that you think deserves a Brief, with a
  link to the primary source.
- **Sponsorship:** newsletter sponsorship enquiries.
- **Other:** anything else about HastCode.

We aim to reply within five working days.
